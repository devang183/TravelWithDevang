Since you’re building a rich, categorized city exploration map on your website, here are some exciting, next-level features you can add to make it more interactive, personal, and engaging for your visitors:

⸻

🔥 1. “My Trail” Feature

Let users build their own itinerary by clicking a ✚ icon next to any location, which adds it to their custom map or list.
	•	Save in localStorage (for now), or user login later.
	•	Add export to PDF or Google Maps.

⸻

🎥 2. Reels / Shorts Embed per Spot

Add short vertical videos (Reels, TikToks, YouTube Shorts) per location.
Show real vibes of the café, beach, or museum.
Can be your own or curated ones.

⸻

🌤️ 3. Live Weather Widget

Show weather for the selected city or even per pin (if you’re ambitious).
Useful for travelers checking beach or park vibes.

⸻

🖼️ 4. Photo Carousel with Captions

Each pin opens a modal with:
	•	A few high-quality photos
	•	Captions like: “Shot this at golden hour from the west entrance.”

⸻

💬 5. Local Tips Section

Per location, a small tip:
	•	Best time to visit
	•	What locals order
	•	Where to sit
	•	Something hidden (e.g. secret rooftop at a pub)

⸻

🧭 6. “Nearby You” Mode (GeoLocation)

If user allows geolocation, highlight 5 closest places from each category.
Useful for mobile explorers in the city.

⸻

🖌️ 7. Street Art Trail Mode

For category:'art', let users “Follow the trail”:
	•	Draw a walking path connecting murals or galleries
	•	Include popups with artist info

⸻

🕵️ 8. Hidden Gems Toggle

A magical toggle: ✨ Show Hidden Gems Only
Filter only the unique, underrated spots (you’ve already collected them well!).

⸻

🌙 9. Night Mode Adventures

Theme flips dark, and you show:
	•	Night cafés, 24hr pharmacies, pubs, viewpoints
	•	Ideal for users out and about at night

⸻

📅 10. Event Calendar

Tie places with upcoming events (e.g. live music at a bar, book signings at a store, pop-up art).

⸻
